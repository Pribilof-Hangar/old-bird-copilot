Old Bird Copilot - spoken copilot callouts for Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024.

Freeware. For entertainment only - not for real-world aviation, navigation, training
or any safety-critical use. An independent project, not affiliated with, endorsed by,
sponsored by or supported by Microsoft, Asobo Studio or PMDG.

What this installs:
- The app, for your Windows user only (no administrator rights needed).
- A Start menu entry (and a desktop shortcut if you tick it).

Nothing is added to the simulator or its Community folder, and nothing else needs to
be installed. The app only reads information from the simulator; it never changes
your aircraft or sends anything over the internet.

Your settings and flight recordings are kept in your user folder
(%APPDATA%\Old Bird Copilot). Updates keep them; uninstalling asks first.
